Our story

Built with a low-end phone, free tools, and a refusal to quit.

No laptop. No investors. No team.

Just a phone, free tools, limited internet — and the belief that great software isn't built by money, it's built by people who refuse to quit.

The nights the code broke

There were sleepless nights, bugs that wouldn't budge, and moments when frustration whispered that it was time to quit. Every fix became another lesson.

Every delay, another chapter

Every bug fixed was a lesson. Every failure was a chapter. Every delay was preparing the ground for something bigger.

Hidex was born

Proof that someone with almost nothing could still build something that mattered — a private, secure place for people to actually connect.

"Today I am a total joke, but tomorrow is mine."

— written the night Hidex almost didn't happen
Benjamin Zigwana
Founder & Developer, Hidex
Why Hidex

Everything a conversation needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Real-time chat

One-on-one conversations that arrive instantly, with read receipts and typing indicators when you want them.

Groups & channels

Bring a community together in one place — group chats for close teams, channels for broadcasting to everyone.

Secure by design

End-to-end encryption keeps conversations between the people in them — not stored, sold, or skimmed.

Built for low-end devices

Designed and tested to stay fast and light, even on older phones and slower connections.
